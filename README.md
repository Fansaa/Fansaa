I'm **Fathan Fardian Sanum**, an Informatics undergraduate student at **Telkom University, Bandung**. My primary interest lies at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and practical software engineering. I have a strong foundation in machine learning concepts, particularly in **Computer Vision** and **Natural Language Processing**, and I enjoy applying these skills to solve real-world problems through well-structured applications.

I am currently conducting academic research on **Isolated Character Recognition for Balinese, Javanese, and Sundanese Script (Aksara)** using deep learning architectures such as **EfficientNet** and **MobileNet**. Beyond research, I actively develop full-stack web applications, with a focus on clean architecture, intuitive UI/UX, and iterative product improvement. I thrive in collaborative environments and believe that good engineering is as much about communication and adaptability as it is about writing clean code.
